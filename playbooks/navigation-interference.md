# Playbook: Navigation and timing interference

Related techniques: EFX-03, MNP-09, EFX-12

Use in an exercise first. Adapt to your mission's procedures and authority before relying on it.

## Steps

1. Confirm from independent sources: multiple receivers, other constellations, inertial or network time.
2. Classify: loss of signal suggests jamming; a consistent wrong answer suggests spoofing.
3. Switch dependent systems to hold-over or alternative sources.
4. Warn users whose safety depends on the service.
5. Record spectrum and receiver data for reporting and geolocation.
6. Report through the national spectrum authority and sector channels.
7. Return to satellite navigation only after cross-checks agree for a sustained period.

## Measure

- Time to detect
- Hold-over duration achieved
- Users warned before acting on false data
